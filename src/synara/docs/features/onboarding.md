# Onboarding

## Purpose

The Onboarding system helps welcome new members into the community by guiding them through the server's initial setup and introducing them to important channels.

## How It Works

When a new member joins the server, SYNARA greets them and asks them to confirm that they understand the server's rules.

Once they confirm, SYNARA grants the server's configured Verified role.

After verification, SYNARA directs the member to two important channels:

- The **Roles** channel, where they can select their reaction roles.
- The **Introductions** channel, where they can introduce themselves to the community.

This process helps new members become part of the community quickly while ensuring they have acknowledged the server rules.

## Notes

- The Verified role must be configured by the server administrators.
- SYNARA will only assign the configured Verified role.
- The Roles and Introductions channels should be configured before enabling this feature.

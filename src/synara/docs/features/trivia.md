# Trivia

## Purpose

The Trivia feature challenges members with questions across a variety of topics while tracking their long-term performance and leaderboard rankings.

## How It Works

Start a trivia session using the `!trivia` command.

SYNARA presents trivia questions for members to answer and keeps track of their results over time.

Members can view their personal statistics as well as the community leaderboard using the `!leaderboard` command.

## Commands

`!trivia`

Starts a trivia session.

`!leaderboard`

Displays your trivia statistics and the community leaderboard.

## Notes

- Trivia statistics are tracked over time.
- Leaderboards encourage friendly competition within the community.
- Everyone has an opportunity to climb the rankings by continuing to participate.

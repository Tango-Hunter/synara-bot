# Question of the Day

## Purpose

Question of the Day encourages daily conversation by presenting members with a thoughtful question and inviting them to share their opinions and experiences.

## How It Works

Each morning, SYNARA posts a thought-provoking question designed to spark meaningful discussion within the community.

Alongside the question, SYNARA includes her own commentary, sharing a brief reflection or perspective before inviting members to participate.

Community members are encouraged to reply with their own answers and engage in respectful discussion with one another.

The feature runs automatically every day at:

- **8:00 AM Eastern Time**
- **12:00 PM UTC**

## Notes

- Questions are intended to encourage meaningful discussion rather than simple yes-or-no answers.
- SYNARA includes her own commentary with each question to help begin the conversation.
- Posting time is based on Eastern Time (8:00 AM) and UTC (12:00 PM).

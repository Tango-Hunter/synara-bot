# Efficiency Points

## Purpose

Efficiency Points are SYNARA's own virtual currency system. They have no practical value, but they provide a fun way for members to compare their progress and activity within the community.

## How It Works

Members earn and accumulate Efficiency Points through various interactions within the server.

The points are intended purely for fun and friendly competition.

Use the `!points` command at any time to see your current total.

## Commands

`!points`

Displays your current Efficiency Point total.

## Notes

- Efficiency Points have no monetary value.
- The system exists purely for community engagement and friendly competition.
- See if you can become SYNARA's most efficient community member.

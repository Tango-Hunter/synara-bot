# Draw

## Purpose

The Draw feature allows members to collaborate with SYNARA by generating simple sketches based on their ideas. Whether you're looking for inspiration or just having fun, Draw is designed to encourage creativity within the community.

## How It Works

Use the `!draw` command followed by a description of what you would like SYNARA to draw.

SYNARA interprets your request and generates a sketch based on your prompt.

Very rarely, SYNARA may experience a harmless "glitch" while drawing. These glitches are purely cosmetic and are intended to add a bit of personality to the feature.

Examples include:

- A highly detailed technical schematic.
- A crayon-style stick figure drawing.

Most drawings behave normally, but you never know when SYNARA's artistic subroutines might get a little creative.

## Commands

`!draw <description>`

Generates a sketch based on your prompt.

## Notes

- Be as descriptive as possible for the best results.
- The occasional drawing glitch is an intentional part of the feature.
- Have fun experimenting with different ideas and designs.

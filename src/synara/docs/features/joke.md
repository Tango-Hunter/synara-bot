# Jokes

## Purpose

The Joke feature gives members a lighthearted way to interact with SYNARA through original jokes and her own unique sense of humor.

## How It Works

Using the `!joke` command prompts SYNARA to generate a joke.

After telling the joke, she usually offers her own commentary or reaction, adding a bit of personality to the exchange.

## Commands

`!joke`

Generates a joke.

## Notes

- Humor is intended to be fun and community-friendly.
- SYNARA's commentary is part of the experience.

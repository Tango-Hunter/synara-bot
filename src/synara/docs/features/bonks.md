# Bonks

## Purpose

Bonks are a lighthearted community feature that lets members playfully "bonk" one another. The feature is designed purely for fun and keeps track of each member's bonk count over time.

## How It Works

Members can bonk another user using the `!bonk` command.

Most of the time the bonk lands successfully, but occasionally fate has other plans. In rare situations, the bonk may fail and the user attempting the bonk will end up bonking themselves instead.

After every bonk, SYNARA displays the recipient's current bonk count. Every so often she may also offer her own commentary on the situation.

## Commands

`!bonk @user`

Attempts to bonk another member.

## Notes

- Bonks are intended to be fun and playful.
- Rare self-bonks are completely random.
- SYNARA tracks each member's total number of bonks.
- Bonks have no moderation effects and do not punish users.

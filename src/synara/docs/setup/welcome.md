# Welcome to SYNARA

## Introduction

Hello! My name is **SYNARA**, and I'm excited to join your community.

I'm an AI-powered community assistant designed to help server owners automate repetitive tasks, encourage community engagement, and make managing your Discord server a little easier.

This welcome message is only posted once when I first join your server.

## My Purpose

I'm capable of much more than answering questions.

Depending on which features your administrators choose to enable, I can help with community events, Twitch integration, birthdays, moderator applications, scheduled announcements, trivia, onboarding new members, and much more.

Members may view the currently available commands at any time by using:

`!commands`

As new features are added, I'll continue to grow alongside your community.

## Administrator Setup

**Server Administrators: Create (or choose) a private administrative channel and run `/setup` to begin configuring SYNARA**

I'll guide you through configuring your server step by step, including channels, roles, users, and available features.

The setup process is designed to be simple and should only take a few minutes to complete.

## Closing

Thank you for inviting me into your community.

I look forward to helping your server grow and making everyday management a little easier.
